Here are my 10 most recent research articles:

- [**Polysynaptic signal propagation in networked neural masses**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.29.721638v1) - We study how polysynaptic signalling emerges in three widely-used neural mass models by tracking a discrete, focal perturbation as it propagates across a network, finding that this effect emerges when the models are tuned to regimes that enhance the persistence of perturbations.

- **Arousal-driven parametric fluctuations augment computational models of dynamic functional connectivity** (Journal of Computational Neuroscience, 54, Suppl. 1) - A conference proceedings abstract extending our critical roaming work, showing how letting arousal parametrically drive a whole-brain model improves its match to empirical dynamic functional connectivity.

- [**Dancing to the beats of the mammalian connectome: Topological and dynamical asymmetries shape infra-slow oscillations**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.01.735524.abstract) - Using coupled oscillator networks embedded in human and macaque connectomes, we show that infra-slow, structured fluctuations in network engagement arise from a common dynamical motif shaped by the topology of the underlying connectome.

- [**The critical roaming hypothesis: arousal-driven transitions across critical lines reproduce human functional connectivity dynamics**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.29.696846v1.abstract) - Our pre-print where we explore the role of arousal in modulating on-going functional connectivity.

- [**Inhibition of thalamic relay nuclei scales the aperiodic and alpha band oscillations associated with arousal during naturalistic stimulus viewing**](https://direct.mit.edu/imag/article/doi/10.1162/imag_a_00451/127395) - We show that arousal during naturalistic viewing is tracked by a rise in the aperiodic EEG exponent/offset and a drop in alpha power, and use a corticothalamic neural field model to trace both effects to stronger inhibitory drive onto thalamic relay nuclei.

- [**The virtual multiple sclerosis patient**](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(24)01326-9) - We integrate diffusion tensor imaging and MEG into individualized virtual brain models to estimate conduction velocities in MS patients versus controls, offering a route past the clinical-radiological paradox that standard tract-specific measures miss.

- [**Emotion arousal but not valence is strongly represented in aperiodic EEG activity stemming from thalamocortical interactions**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.03.11.584477v1) - Using the DEAP dataset, we find that the aperiodic EEG exponent and offset track emotional arousal but not valence, and use a thalamocortical neural field model to show this stems from enhanced inhibitory coupling between thalamic reticular and relay populations.

- [**Metastability indexes global changes in the dynamic working point of the brain following brain stimulation**](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurorobotics/articles/10.3389/fnbot.2024.1336438/full) - We characterize how single-pulse TMS transiently reduces metastability and increases coherence in global brain network dynamics, with higher EEG frequencies recovering faster than lower ones, offering a way to quantify how long stimulation effects linger.

- [**Biophysical mechanism underlying compensatory preservation of neural synchrony over the adult lifespan**](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-022-03489-4) - Here we combine dynamical systems modelling with MEG analysis to understand age-related neural compensations.

- [**Whole-Brain Network Models: From Physics to Bedside**](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2022.866517/full) - Here we review the field of large scale whole brain computational models.
